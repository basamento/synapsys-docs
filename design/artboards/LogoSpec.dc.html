Nucleus

A soma with three axons leaving it, each ending in a terminal. The mark draws the architecture: one Core, many neurons reaching out from it. Terminals are always teal — they are the part that means “worker”.

There is no lockup SVG on purpose. The wordmark is live Bricolage Grotesque, so the lockup is assembled in markup — that keeps it selectable, translatable and crisp at any size. A flattened lockup would only be needed for print, and would want the type outlined by an illustrator.
