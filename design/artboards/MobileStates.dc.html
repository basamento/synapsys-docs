BREAKPOINTS
996px — Docusaurus’s own. Sidebar becomes a drawer, TOC hides. Do not invent a different one for the docs shell.
640px — the landing page’s own. Grids collapse to one column, buttons go full width.
TAP TARGETS
Anything tappable is at least 44px tall below 996px — sidebar rows, navbar icons, footer links, tabs.
The desktop boards use 30px rows. That is fine with a mouse and fails on a phone, so tokens.css raises them at the breakpoint rather than making desktop loose.
OVERFLOW
Wide content scrolls inside its own container. The page body never scrolls sideways.
Infima does not do this for tables — a wide table will push the whole page. The rule in tokens.css is what stops it.
